## 👋 About Me

I'm **Shiju Reji**, a Flutter Developer with **2+ years** of experience shipping production cross-platform mobile apps. Currently building at **[Notespedia](https://github.com/Shiju738)**, where I turn product requirements into clean, performant, well-architected apps for Android & iOS.

- 🧱 I build with **Clean Architecture** and **MVVM** — using **Riverpod, BLoC, GetX & Provider** depending on the project's needs.
- 📱 I care about **pixel-perfect UI, smooth animations, and offline-first** experiences.
- 🔌 Comfortable across the stack: **Firebase, Supabase, REST APIs, SQLite & Hive**.
- 🚀 Recent work spans **P2P Bluetooth messaging, inventory management systems, and health-tech apps**.
- 💬 Ask me about **Flutter, Dart, state management & mobile app architecture**.
